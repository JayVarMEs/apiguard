## C. Method weaknesses (say them in Threats, or fix)

1. **Five seeds are not five different splits.** `split_groups` places the big providers the same way every time; about 87% of endpoints sit in the same fold for every seed. The "mean of five seeds" spread is too small. Fix: shuffle providers fully per seed, or report it as a limitation.
2. **Hyperparameters are tuned once (seed 0) and reused for seeds 1-4**, whose test folds were in that tuning data. Small leak.
3. **Group C (data sensitivity) carries no signal**: lowest SHAP (0.06) and PR-AUC goes *up* when it is removed (0.44 to 0.46). It is described as the main thing a rule cannot see. The paper hides this in "other groups changed it by at most 0.04".
4. **The best result (E8, scanner + model) is not what the tool does.** The CLI gates on the model score alone.
5. **tau is tuned on scanner labels** in E1 (validation folds are mostly L1), then F1/MCC are scored on L2.
6. **Precision@k is unweighted** on a stratified sample, while every other metric is weighted.
7. **Scanner attribution is too wide**: any finding outside `paths` (for example in `components/schemas`) is given to every endpoint. This is why 98% of endpoints are flagged and 21 rules had to be dropped, including `read-restricted` (the cause found in E6). Better: give a schema finding only to endpoints that use that schema.
8. **5 files that Spectral could not scan stay in the dataset** as "no findings, label 0".
9. E2 "best case" reference model is trained on L2 category labels only, so it is not a real best case. E2 uses one seed.
10. Category labels are the union over passes, so API2 covers 54 of 62 positives. E2 for API2 is close to the whole task.
11. "Over 85% flagged without this" is the 25% cut-off run; with no cut-off it is 98%.
